# Product-to-R&D handoff schema

## 1. Manifest

- Product / initiative
- Requirement ID and version
- Handoff date
- Product owner
- Receiving R&D owner or agent
- Readiness: READY / READY WITH CONDITIONS / NOT READY
- Canonical artifact paths and versions

## 2. Outcome and scope

- User problem and target outcome
- In scope
- Out of scope
- Success measures

## 3. Traceability matrix

| User goal | Requirement ID | Flow/page | State or rule | Acceptance ID | Artifact |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |

## 4. Product contract

- Roles and permissions
- Business rules and validations
- Critical flows
- State matrix and recovery behavior
- Product-level data inputs/outputs
- Product-level non-functional expectations

## 5. UI and prototype

- Design-system version
- Prototype version and artifact state
- Supported viewports/themes
- Known mock behavior and production-equivalent expectations
- Audit report and browser evidence

## 6. Decisions and uncertainty

| ID | Type | Statement | Evidence / reason | Owner | Due / status |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |

Types: fact, approved decision, assumption, open question, accepted risk, deferred scope.

## 7. R&D decision boundary

- R&D may decide independently
- Product confirmation required
- Explicit technical questions for the receiving agent

## 8. Readiness conclusion

- Result
- Conditions or blockers
- Required next action
- Acceptance owner
